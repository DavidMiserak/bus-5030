# Managerial Accounting and Control Concepts (BUS-5030) [3 credits]

## Course Description

This course will teach you to read, interpret, and analyze financial statements, applying financial and managerial accounting concepts, tools, and techniques to enhance strategic management thinking. Emphasizing a cross-functional perspective, the course integrates internal accounting systems with managerial decision-making across various organizational functions.

## Course Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, you shall be able to:

1. Evaluate organizational control systems by analyzing responsibility
   centers, performance measures, incentive structures, and behavioral
   consequences to determine their effectiveness in aligning
   managerial decisions with organizational objectives.
1. Analyze and interpret financial and nonfinancial performance
   measures using advanced managerial accounting tools, including
   variance analysis, responsibility accounting, balanced scorecards,
   and key performance indicators, to diagnose operational and
   strategic performance issues.
1. Design and justify management control mechanisms for decentralized
   organizations by integrating transfer pricing, budgeting,
   performance evaluation, and incentive compensation concepts
   while considering goal congruence and dysfunctional behavioral
   effects.
1. Assess managerial decisions under conditions of uncertainty and
   competing objectives by applying relevant cost analysis, budgeting,
   quantitative performance information, and professional judgment to
   recommend and defend appropriate courses of action.
1. Critically evaluate the ethical and strategic implications of
   managerial accounting controls by identifying how performance
   targets, measurement systems, and incentive arrangements can
   influence managerial behavior, organizational culture, decision
   quality, and long-term value creation.

# Personal Introduction

Imagine you've just been promoted to manage your favorite restaurant, sports team, coffee shop, clothing store, or entertainment venue. Congratulations, you're in charge!

Your boss gives you one simple instruction: "Make this place more successful.""

For your introduction, share:

## What organization did you choose, and why?

I chose to manage a Starbucks coffee shop because I have always been passionate about coffee culture and creating a welcoming environment for customers. Starbucks is known for its strong brand and customer loyalty, and I believe I can contribute to enhancing the overall experience for both customers and employees.

## What three things would you track to determine whether you're doing a good job?

1. Window time (drive-thru service speed)
2. Connection score (customer satisfaction 1-5)
3. Pour cost and pastry waste

## Would all three involve money, or would you measure other things such as customer satisfaction, quality, employee performance, wait times, or productivity?

Window time and pour cost/pastry waste would involve money, while the Connection score would measure customer satisfaction. I believe it's important to track both financial metrics and customer experience to ensure the overall success of the coffee shop.

## What is one rule, reward, or incentive you would introduce to encourage your team to perform well?

I would allow the top employee of the month to list a custom drink on the menu for a week. This would not only motivate employees to perform well but also encourage creativity and engagement with the brand.

## What could go wrong if employees focused too much on the measures or incentives you selected?

Possible issues could arise if employees focus too much on the measures or incentives. For example, if they prioritize speed over quality to improve window time, it could lead to mistakes in orders or a decline in customer satisfaction. Similarly, if they are overly focused on reducing pour cost and pastry waste, they might compromise on the quality of the products served. It's important to maintain a balance between efficiency and quality to ensure long-term success.

## Share something about yourself that feels right to you

I am a huge Tabletop Role-Playing Game (TTRPG) fan and I am very excited for the upcoming release of the Dungeon Crawler Carl system. I enjoy immersing myself in different worlds and storytelling, and TTRPGs provide a unique way to connect with friends and explore creativity.

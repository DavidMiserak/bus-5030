#+TITLE: Week 1 Project Assignment

* Writing Assignment Overview: Upstream, Midstream, and Downstream Costs in the Oil and Gas Industry

The oil and gas industry is commonly divided into three major
operational segments: upstream, midstream, and downstream. Each
segment performs a distinct economic function and has a different cost
structure, risk profile, capital requirement, and exposure to market
conditions. Understanding these differences is essential for managers,
investors, policymakers, and energy professionals responsible for
evaluating projects and making strategic decisions.

* Task: Introduce, Analyze, Explain and Evaluate

For this assignment, write a 750–1,000-word analytical essay in APA
7th edition examining the major costs associated with upstream,
midstream, and downstream oil and gas operations. Your analysis should
move beyond simply defining each segment. Evaluate why costs differ
across the three segments, what factors drive those costs, and how
changes in costs influence profitability and strategic
decision-making.

* Criteria: Required Components
1) Introduction
   + Briefly introduce the oil and gas value chain and explain the
     relationship among upstream, midstream, and downstream
     activities. Establish a clear thesis statement identifying the
     primary argument of your essay regarding differences in cost
     structures and their strategic importance.

2) Upstream Costs
   + Analyze 1-2 major costs associated with the exploration and
     production of oil and natural gas. Your discussion should
     consider costs such as:
     - Geological and geophysical exploration
     - Lease acquisition and mineral rights
     - Drilling and well completion
     - Labor, equipment, and oilfield services
     - Production and lifting costs
     - Water management and disposal
     - Well maintenance and workovers
     - Environmental compliance
     - Plugging, abandonment, and site restoration

3) Midstream Costs
   + Evaluate the 1-2 costs associated with transporting, gathering, processing, storing, and distributing oil and natural gas. Consider:
     - Pipeline construction
     - Gathering systems
     - Compressor and pumping stations
     - Natural gas processing
     - Storage facilities
     - Transportation and terminal infrastructure
     - Pipeline integrity and maintenance
     - Energy consumption
     - Regulatory and environmental compliance

Analyze why midstream operations often involve large initial capital
investments but relatively predictable operating costs once
infrastructure is in service. Discuss how throughput volume,
transportation contracts, utilization rates, distance, and regulatory
requirements influence the economics of midstream assets.

4. Downstream Costs
   + Analyze 1 or 2 principal costs involved in refining crude oil and
     marketing petroleum products. Your discussion may include:
     - Crude oil feedstock
     - Refinery operations
     - Energy and utilities
     - Labor
     - Maintenance and turnaround expenses
     - Transportation and product distribution
     - Environmental compliance
     - Product blending
     - Storage
     - Retail and marketing expenses

5. Conclusion

Summarize the most significant differences among upstream, midstream,
and downstream costs. Your conclusion should explain why understanding
these cost structures is important for capital allocation,
profitability analysis, strategic planning, and risk management in the
energy industry.

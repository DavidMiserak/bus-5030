## Scenario

Act as a financial analyst evaluating a Fortune 500 company for potential
investors. Build a financial analysis and determine how sensitive the
company's financial performance or valuation is to changes in important
assumptions.

Select **one publicly traded Fortune 500 company** with sufficient financial
information available through annual reports, SEC filings, investor-relations
materials, and other credible sources.

- Analyze financial performance by interpreting key financial statements,
  ratios, and operating metrics.
- Identify key financial and operational variables that materially influence
  profitability, cash flow, and valuation.
- Conduct sensitivity and scenario analyses across assumptions such as revenue
  growth, operating margins, costs, and discount rates.
- Interpret and communicate results using models, tables, charts, and
  evidence-based explanations.
- Evaluate financial risk and support decision-making by assessing business
  resilience.

### 5. Interpretation

Analyze rather than restate the calculations. Address:

#### Which assumption has the greatest impact?

The majority of Costco's operating income comes from membership fees, which are nearly pure profit. Therefore, the renewal rate of membership fees has the greatest impact on the company's financial performance. A small change in the renewal rate can significantly affect operating income and overall profitability.

#### Which has the least?

Fixed costs have the least impact on Costco's financial performance. Since fixed costs remain constant regardless of sales volume, variations in fixed costs do not significantly affect the company's operating income or profitability.

#### How much does valuation or profitability shift between downside and upside?

With a 5% decrease in net sales, EPS drops from $18.21 to $17.15, representing a decline of approximately 5.9%. Conversely, with a 5% increase in net sales, EPS rises to $19.27, an increase of about 5.8%. This indicates that profitability is moderately sensitive to changes in net sales, highlighting the importance of maintaining strong sales performance.

#### What do the results say about financial risk?

Costco's financial risk is relatively low due to its strong membership fee revenue, which provides a stable and predictable income stream. However, the sensitivity analysis shows that the company is vulnerable to changes in key variables such as membership renewal rates and revenue growth. Investors should be aware of these risks and monitor them closely to assess potential impacts on profitability.

#### Which variables should investors or managers monitor most closely?

Membership renewal rates and revenue growth should be monitored most closely. These variables have the greatest impact on Costco's financial performance and profitability. Any significant changes in these areas could materially affect the company's operating income and overall financial health.

### 6. Conclusion

Summarize findings and state whether the company appears financially resilient
or vulnerable to changes in key assumptions. From an investor's perspective,
give a final **Buy, Hold, or Sell** recommendation supported by the
sensitivity results.

I would recommend a **Hold** for Costco Wholesale Corporation. The company demonstrates financial resilience due to its strong membership fee revenue and low fixed costs. However, the sensitivity analysis indicates that small changes in key variables, particularly membership renewal rates and revenue growth, can significantly impact profitability. Investors should monitor these variables closely to assess potential risks and opportunities.

investors. Build a financial analysis and determine how sensitive the

information available through annual reports, SEC filings, investor-relations

### 4. Sensitivity Analysis

Develop at least three scenarios:

- **Downside case** — assumptions less favorable than expected
- **Base case** — most reasonable expectations
- **Upside case** — assumptions more favorable than expected

Vary the selected variables and calculate the effect on financial results or
valuation. **At least one sensitivity table is required** (e.g., combinations
of revenue growth and operating margin against EPS, free cash flow, or
estimated share value).

#### Base

Tariffs remain at $1.5B, revenue growth flat at 0.2%, operating margin at 8.2%.

#### Upside

Tariffs reduced to $0.5B, revenue growth +3%, operating margin at 9.5%.

#### Downside

Tariffs increase to $2.0B, revenue growth −3%, operating margin at 7.0%.

### 5. Interpretation

Analyze rather than restate the calculations. Address:

#### Which assumption has the greatest impact?

Tariffs have the most significant effect on gross margin and net income, as they directly influence the cost of goods sold. A $1B change in tariffs can swing operating income by hundreds of millions, highlighting the sensitivity of profitability to trade policies.

#### Which has the least?

Free cash flow is less sensitive to small changes in revenue growth, as the company maintains strong cash management practices. Minor fluctuations in revenue do not drastically affect cash flow due to operational efficiencies and cost controls.

#### How much does valuation or profitability shift between downside and upside?

Tariff scenarios can lead to a swing of over $1B in net income, which translates to significant changes in EPS and potentially the stock price. The difference between the downside and upside cases could represent a 20-30% variation in profitability, indicating substantial financial risk.

#### What do the results say about financial risk?

The results indicate that NIKE is vulnerable to external factors such as tariffs and global trade policies. While the company has a strong brand and market position, its profitability is highly sensitive to cost structures and revenue growth. Investors should be cautious of macroeconomic conditions that could exacerbate these risks.

#### Which variables should investors or managers monitor most closely?

Tariffs, revenue growth, and operating margins should be closely monitored. Changes in trade policies, consumer demand, and competitive pressures can significantly impact these variables, affecting overall financial performance.

### 6. Conclusion

Summarize findings and state whether the company appears financially resilient
or vulnerable to changes in key assumptions. From an investor's perspective,
give a final **Buy, Hold, or Sell** recommendation supported by the
sensitivity results.

While a recognized leader in the athletic apparel industry, NIKE's financial performance is highly sensitive to external factors such as tariffs and revenue growth. The sensitivity analysis indicates that while the company has strong fundamentals, it faces significant risks that could impact profitability. Given the current market conditions and the potential for volatility in key variables, a **Hold** recommendation is prudent, allowing investors to monitor how NIKE navigates these challenges before making further investment decisions.
